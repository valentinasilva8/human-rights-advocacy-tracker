## Two actions, not one

The sources require two records.

1. Withdrawal. KPH says the cassation of the District Prosecutor in Płock was effectively withdrawn. rp.pl says the Płock prosecution withdrew its cassation in recent days. Neither page states the day. This is one procedural action shared by the three defendant rows. It is not counted as a judicial decision, and it is not a dismissal.
2. Dismissal. One reported decision on 28 March 2024 dismissed, as obviously groundless, the cassations of counsel for auxiliary prosecutors Kaja Godek and Tadeusz Łebkowski. Those are two challengers in one disposition. They are not two decisions. rp.pl names Kaja Godek and the Płock priest. It does not spell Tadeusz Łebkowski. The name Łebkowski is KPH’s.

Amnesty Slovakia’s sentence that the Supreme Court rejected the state authorities’ appeal collapses these actions. It is not the proposed claim.

KPH’s page uses both `postanowienie` and, later, `wyrok`. This packet uses “decision” and does not resolve the document form. The decision was not inspected.

The closed event list has no cassation type. The withdrawal rows use `Appeal withdrawn`. The dismissal rows use `Appeal decided`. The procedural stage on each row says which cassation action it is. Neither type is the 12 January 2022 regional-court appeal.

Page date: 28 March 2024. Read 10 October 2026.

Source id: `pl_src_kph`. Provenance on the proposed rows: `unknown`. Scope: `whole_claim`, because the stored sentence is KPH’s account. That is not an independent count.

**Original passage.** “Dzisiaj (28 marca) w Sądzie Najwyższym odbyła się rozprawa kasacyjna ws. trzech aktywistek oskarżonych o obrazę uczuć religijnych poprzez rozwieszanie naklejek z wizerunkiem matki boskiej częstochowskiej z tęczową aureolą. Sąd oddalił kasacje złożone przez pełnomocników ubocznych oskarżycieli posiłkowych, a kasacja Prokuratora Rejonowego w Płocku została skutecznie cofnięta. Sąd Najwyższy uznał, iż kasacje wniesione przez pełnomocników Kai Godek i Tadeusza Łebkowskiego są bezzasadne w stopniu oczywistym (postanowienie Sądu Najwyższego z dnia 28 marca 2024 r., sygn. akt V KK 430/22).”

**English translation.** Today (28 March) a cassation hearing was held in the Supreme Court in the case of three activists accused of offending religious feelings by putting up stickers of Our Lady of Częstochowa with a rainbow halo. The court dismissed the cassations filed by counsel for the auxiliary prosecutors, and the cassation of the District Prosecutor in Płock was effectively withdrawn. The Supreme Court found that the cassations brought by counsel for Kaja Godek and Tadeusz Łebkowski were obviously groundless (Supreme Court decision of 28 March 2024, case V KK 430/22; the page’s word is postanowienie).

**Original passage, same proceeding.** “Elżbieta Podleśna, Joanna Gzyra-Iskandar i Anna Prus rozwiesiły w okolicy kościoła naklejki z wizerunkiem matki boskiej częstochowskiej z tęczową aureolą.” “Sądy dwóch instancji (wyrok Sądu Rejonowego w Płocku z 2.03.2021 r. oraz Sądu Okręgowego w Płocku z 12.01.2022 r.) uniewinniły aktywistki w całości od zarzucanego im czynu.”

**English translation.** Elżbieta Podleśna, Joanna Gzyra-Iskandar, and Anna Prus put up stickers of Our Lady of Częstochowa with a rainbow halo near the church. Courts of two instances (the judgment of the District Court in Płock of 2 March 2021 and of the Regional Court in Płock of 12 January 2022) acquitted the activists in full of the act charged against them.

**Original passage, later wording on the same page.** “W dzisiejszym wyroku Sąd Najwyższy oddalił kasacje pełnomocników ubocznych oskarżycieli posiłkowych (Kai Godek i Tadeusza Łebkowskiego), złożone od wyroku sądu II instancji.”

**English translation.** In today’s judgment the Supreme Court dismissed the cassations of counsel for the auxiliary prosecutors (Kaja Godek and Tadeusz Łebkowski), filed against the second-instance judgment.

**Original passage, KPH’s role.** “KPH bierze udział w sprawie jako strona społeczna i jest reprezentowane przez adw. Marcina Pawelca-Jakowieckiego.”

**English translation.** KPH takes part in the case as a social party and is represented by advocate Marcin Pawelec-Jakowiecki.

**English translation.** This follows the Supreme Court’s decision, which on Thursday dismissed as obviously groundless the cassations in this case of Kaja Godek and the Płock priest. Three activists reacted: Elżbieta Podleśna, Joanna Gzyra-Iskandar, and Anna Prus. In March 2021 the District Court in Płock acquitted the women. The acquitting judgment was upheld in January 2022 by the Płock regional court. In recent days the Płock prosecution withdrew its cassation. The Supreme Court ultimately dismissed the remaining cassations on Thursday as obviously groundless. Case signature: V KK 430/22.

“W ostatnich dniach” means “in recent days.” It is not a calendar date. Thursday is 28 March 2024, the day of the dismissal and the publication day. The publication timestamp 14:43 is not the withdrawal’s event date.
