# Cross-case learning, after several reviewed cases

This is the next phase. It is not part of the current build. The current build is one evidence tracker: what was argued, by whom, through which intervention, what documented response exists, and what happened afterward.

When several cases have been approved by a person, the next work is to compare them. The comparison asks:

- Whether the cases can be compared at all, by proceeding, legal issue, intervention type, and timing.
- Which arguments received explicit discussion, adoption, or rejection, and where the decision was not retrieved or the reasoning was insufficient.
- What subsequent developments and implemented remedies are documented, with their dates and sources.
- Where the evidence is missing or where sources contradict each other.

The strength of the documentation is not the effectiveness of the advocacy. A later acquittal, a upheld conviction, a UN opinion, and an implemented remedy stay separate events. None of them proves that an argument worked or failed.

This phase does not add a success score, a ranking, or a list of winning arguments. The corpus is small and selected. A pattern is a question for a person to investigate.

Lawyer-facing research and drafting can be considered only after that comparison exists. They are not implied by the tracker.
